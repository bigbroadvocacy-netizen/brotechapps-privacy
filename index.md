# Big Bro Apps Privacy Policy
Last updated: [03/28/2026]

This Privacy Policy applies to all Big Bro applications created by BroTechApps, including but not limited to Big Bro Advocacy Assistant, Big Bro Planner, and any future Big Bro tools (“Big Bro Apps”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Big Bro Apps are designed to operate without collecting personal data or requiring user accounts. This document explains what information is handled by the apps and how it is used.

---

## 1. Overview
Big Bro Apps prioritize user privacy and safety. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers. All data created within the apps remains on the user’s device unless the user chooses to share it.

---

## 2. Data We Collect
### We do **not** collect:
- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Account or login credentials
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Notes, tasks, planner entries, or any user-generated content

### Local Device Storage
Some apps may store:
- Planner entries
- Notes
- Settings and preferences
- Accessibility options

This information is stored **locally on your device only** and never transmitted to us.

---

## 3. External Links
Big Bro Apps may include optional links to external websites such as:
- YouTube
- .org informational resources
- .gov resources
- Copilot AI tools (translation, search, etc.)

These links are provided for convenience.  
We do not control these websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices.  
Visiting external sites is optional and user‑initiated.

---

## 4. Third‑Party Services
Big Bro Apps do **not** use:
- Analytics services  
- Advertising networks  
- Tracking tools  
- Third‑party SDKs  

If an app uses an external API (e.g., weather data), it is used **without sending personal information**.

---

## 5. Data Sharing
We do **not** share, sell, or transfer any personal data to third parties.

---

## 6. Data Security
Because Big Bro Apps do not collect or store personal data on servers, the primary security responsibility lies with the user’s device.  
We recommend keeping your device updated and protected.

---

## 7. Data Reset and Deletion
Users may delete all app data at any time by:
- Clearing the app’s local storage  
- Using the app’s built‑in reset option (if available)  
- Uninstalling the app  

No account deletion is required because no accounts exist.

---

## 8. No AI Training
Big Bro Apps do **not** collect or transmit user data for AI training.  
Any AI features (such as translation or search) are accessed through external links and are governed by the policies of those services.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy
Big Bro Apps do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.  
If a child uses the apps, no identifiable information is collected.

---

## 10. Terms of Use
By using Big Bro Apps, you agree to:
- Use the apps responsibly  
- Understand that external links lead to third‑party websites  
- Accept that we are not responsible for third‑party content or policies  
- Use the apps “as‑is” without warranties  

Big Bro Apps are provided for informational and organizational purposes only.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Updates will be posted on our GitHub page.
