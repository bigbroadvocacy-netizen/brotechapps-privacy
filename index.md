Privacy Policy for Big Bro Advocacy Assistant

Last updated: [03/27/2026]

Big Bro Advocacy Assistant (“the App”) is developed by BroTechApps.
This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

1. Information We Collect
The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
We do not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details.

2. How the App Works
The App provides guidance, translation, and support features by sending user‑entered text
to Microsoft Copilot (an AI service) for processing. This text is used only to generate
responses and is not stored by the App.

3. Third‑Party Processing
The App may send user‑provided text to Microsoft Copilot or other AI services to generate
answers or translations. These services may temporarily process the text to provide a
response. They operate under their own privacy policies.

The App does not store, transmit, or reuse this information beyond the immediate request.

4. Device Permissions
The App may request basic device permissions (such as internet access) only to allow the
app to function properly. No data from these permissions is collected or transmitted by
the App.

5. Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

6. Data Security
Since the App does not collect or store personal data, no user information is at risk of
unauthorized access from within the App.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the
“Last updated” date above.
