# Big Bro Apps – Support

If you need help with any Big Bro application, you’re in the right place.

---

## 1. Common Support Topics

### • App not saving data
Big Bro Apps store data locally on your device.  
Try:
- Refreshing the app  
- Clearing local storage  
- Restarting your device  

### • Accessibility features not working
Check your device’s system accessibility settings.  
Some features depend on system‑level permissions.

### • External links not opening
Your browser or device may be blocking pop‑ups or external navigation.
